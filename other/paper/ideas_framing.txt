From Tuckete: "[...] in explaining the world, scientists strive for models that both have high predictive power and are interpretable. Similarly, in neuroscience, understanding can come in the form of quantitatively accurate models that can predict how a brain cell, region, or network of regions will respond to a particular stimulus. But understanding can also come in the form of intuitive descriptions of stimulus properties that lead to stronger or weaker neural responses (e.g., 132,133). Here, we have coalesced these two kinds of understanding: we put predictive models to a strong test—assessing their ability to drive neural activity up or down— while, simultaneously, characterizing our stimuli in detail to gain insights into linguistic properties that modulate neural responses in the target brain areas."

Trade-off between desiderata; balance between predictivity and simplicity. However, there must be a substantial increase in predictivity to prefer a more complex and less interpretable model.
