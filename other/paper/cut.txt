Regarding (1), the \textsc{emb} method assumes a degree of isomorphism between the models' representations and brain activity, presupposing that the language models' embeddings can be mapped onto neural activity by virtue of some shared representational properties. \textsc{sfl}, on the other hand, relaxes this premise, and only assumes that the human language processor sensitizes to the probabilistic estimates generated by the language models, potentially relying on different representations.
