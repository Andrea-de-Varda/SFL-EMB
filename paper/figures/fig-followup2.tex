\caption{Correlation patterns between cognitive and linguistic probes. The probes learned on the behavioral (blue) and neural (red) datasets are reported on top, and are connected with the linguistic probes on the right. The black lines connect linguistic and cognitive probes that are significantly correlated ($p < .01$, uncorrected); the blue and red rectangles in the middle report the Pearson correlation coefficients. The behavioral probes are mostly correlated with surface-level probes, whereas the neural probes are associated with several surface, syntactic, and semantic probes.}
